\section*{Method and status}
\markboth{Method and status}{Method and status}

The \CatalogueNativeCount\ \emph{native} records are rendered directly by
\texttt{aper} from
locally encoded \texttt{TilingSystem} objects. Their rule panels contain every
encoded prototile replacement; their patch panels use the fixed seed, depth,
and colour recorded in \texttt{doc/catalogue/systems.tsv}. Structural validity
does not imply that every presentation rule is a disjoint literal polygon
partition.

The remaining bank records are \emph{reference only}. Their metadata and any
available patch or rule artwork come from the Tilings Encyclopedia snapshot
and reference-art manifest. They have not been reconstructed, geometrically
validated, or classified by \texttt{aper}. Missing artwork is printed as an
explicit placeholder; in particular, a missing rule image does not mean that
no mathematical rule exists.

The bounded binary-square search enumerates, validates, canonicalises, and
deduplicates a finite search space. Exact-bank comparison covers only the
local encodings and only canonical rule equality. It does not detect broader
equivalences such as MLD, nor establish novelty, nonperiodicity, or
aperiodicity.

\section*{Reproducibility and licence}

The catalogue build performs no network access. Source files are copied to
sanitised generated paths, their SHA-256 values are verified, and any media
conversion is stated on its plate. The exact native rendering command is also
printed beneath each local image. The source metadata report
\CatalogueReferencePatchCount\ patch images and
\CatalogueReferenceRuleCount\ rule images available for reference-only
records; all other panels are labelled missing.

The Encyclopedia-derived metadata and reference artwork are licensed under
